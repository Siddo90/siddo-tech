siddo_tech publisher

siddo_tech publisher is the channel's own upload tool. When a video is finished and approved, it uploads the video with its title, description and tags to the siddo_tech YouTube channel, and publishes it as a Reel on the siddo_tech Instagram account.

It is used only by the owner of siddo_tech, for siddo_tech's own accounts. It offers no sign-in to anyone else and does not read or store data about viewers. What it accesses and keeps is set out in the privacy policy.
