Privacy policy

For siddo_tech publisher, the upload tool of the siddo_tech channel. Effective 5 November 2026 (Instagram section updated the same day).

Who uses it

siddo_tech publisher is used only by the owner of siddo_tech to publish siddo_tech's own videos to siddo_tech's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to other people, has no user accounts of its own, and collects no information about viewers or anyone else.

What it accesses

YouTube youtube.uploadUploads videos, with their title, description and tags, to the siddo_tech channel.
YouTube youtube.readonlyReads the signed-in channel's name once, to confirm the tool is connected to the right channel.
Instagram instagram_business_basicReads the account's ID and username, to confirm the tool is connected to siddo_tech, and keeps its sign-in token renewed.
Instagram instagram_business_content_publishPublishes Reels, with their caption, to the siddo_tech Instagram account.

To hand a finished video to Instagram, the tool makes that one video file reachable at a temporary, unguessable web address (a Cloudflare tunnel) for the few minutes Instagram takes to fetch it, then closes it. Nothing else is served.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue to the tool, the account IDs they belong to, and the ID and link of each video it has published. These are kept only on computers the channel owner controls (their own PC and home server), are not uploaded anywhere else, and are not shared with, sold to or used by any third party. The tool reads no data from other users' accounts and uses none of the data it accesses for advertising or analytics.

YouTube API Services

siddo_tech publisher uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page.

Meta platforms

Instagram data is handled by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy. Access can be removed in the Instagram app's settings under Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Deleting data

When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and the owner deletes them together with the account IDs. Records of published videos are deleted on request. To ask for deletion, or with any question about this policy, email djsiddo@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
