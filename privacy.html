Privacy policy

For siddo_tech publisher, the upload tool of the siddo_tech channel. Effective 5 November 2026; updated 14 November 2026 to add the statistics the tool reads.

Who uses it

siddo_tech publisher is used only by the owner of siddo_tech to publish siddo_tech's own videos to siddo_tech's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to other people, has no user accounts of its own, and collects no information about viewers or anyone else.

What it accesses

YouTube youtube.uploadUploads videos, with their title, description and tags, to the siddo_tech channel.
YouTube youtube.readonlyReads the signed-in channel's name, to confirm the tool is connected to the right channel, and the privacy status and public counts (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos.
YouTube yt-analytics.readonlyReads YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own videos: views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources, likes, shares and subscribers gained. These are totals per video; YouTube Analytics contains no data about individual viewers.
Instagram instagram_basicReads the account's ID, username and follower count, and the like and comment counts of its own Reels.
Instagram instagram_content_publishPublishes Reels, with their caption, to the siddo_tech Instagram account.
Instagram instagram_manage_insightsReads Instagram Insights for the account's own Reels: views, reach, average watch time, saves and shares. These are totals per Reel, with no data about individual viewers.

Meta's sign-in for this kind of app also grants the token Instagram permissions for comments and messages. The tool never uses them; it only reads the account's ID and username and publishes Reels.

Videos are uploaded to Instagram directly. Where that isn't available, the tool makes that one video file reachable at a temporary, unguessable web address (a Cloudflare tunnel) for the few minutes Instagram takes to fetch it, then closes it. Nothing else is served.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue to the tool, the account IDs they belong to, the ID and link of each video it has published, and dated snapshots of the per-video totals listed above, which the channel owner uses to judge which videos work. These are kept only on computers the channel owner controls (their own PC and home server), are not uploaded anywhere else, and are not shared with, sold to or used by any third party. The tool reads no data from other users' accounts, holds no data about individual viewers, and uses none of the data it accesses for advertising.

YouTube API Services

siddo_tech publisher uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page.

Meta platforms

Instagram data is handled by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy. Access can be removed in the Instagram app's settings under Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Deleting data

When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and the owner deletes them together with the account IDs. Records of published videos are deleted on request. To ask for deletion, or with any question about this policy, email djsiddo@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
